# The Memory Repairman — Outline

A three-chapter supervised sample, designed to be read as a complete opening movement. Each chapter stands on a concrete act of repair; together they arc from professional routine to personal reckoning.

## Chapter 1 — "The Shop on Cinder Lane"

**Function:** Establish world, protagonist, and the cost of the craft through a representative job.

- Open on Elias repairing a memory for an old man who wants his late wife back, exactly as she was.
- Show the loom, the seam, the grey. Elias delivers the technical truth; the client hears only what he wants.
- Establish the rule: no repairing his own engrams. Establish the shop, the Ward, the rain-on-metal smell.
- Inciting object: a sealed engram left at the door, unlabeled, paid in advance, marked *"for E. Vane."*

**End beat:** Elias recognizes his own handwriting on the seal.

## Chapter 2 — "A Client Without a Face"

**Function:** Deepen the mystery; force the choice.

- Elias examines the engram on his loom. It is a memory he erased — the incident that ended his career as an architect.
- He cannot decode it without entering it; entering it means repairing it; repairing it breaks the rule.
- Flashback fragments: the lab, the first commercial loom, the blue door.
- He argues with himself in the voice of his former mentor. Decides to proceed.

**End beat:** He threads the first synthetic seam into his own memory and wakes with rain-smell and a name he shouldn't know.

## Chapter 3 — "The Price of Repair"

**Function:** Climax and thematic resolution.

- Inside the memory: the lab, the blue door, the decision he made years ago — the choice to sell the technology knowing what the grey would do.
- The memory offers him a repaired version: himself as innocent, the incident as someone else's fault. Clean. Warmthless.
- He refuses the comfortable fill. Leaves the fracture raw. Exits the loom with the truth intact and unbearable.
- Final image: he places the unrepairable engram in the drawer with the rule he wrote, and opens the shop for the next client.

**End beat:** The old man from Chapter 1 returns, wanting his wife "fixed" again. Elias says: *"Tell me what she actually did."*
