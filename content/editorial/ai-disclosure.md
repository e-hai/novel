# How this story was made

*The Memory Repairman* is a work of speculative fiction. It was drafted with the assistance of an AI language model, then reviewed, restructured, and edited by a human editor before publication.

- **Concept, world-building, and outline:** developed by the human editor, with AI used to expand and challenge ideas.
- **Prose:** initial drafts generated by AI, then substantially rewritten by the human editor for voice, consistency, and pacing.
- **Editorial control:** the human editor made all final decisions on plot, character, and wording. No chapter was published without human review.

There is no attempt to present this work as though it were written solely by a human, and no fabricated author biography or portrait is used. The author is identified honestly as the human editor responsible for the work.

## Classical-fiction fusion edition (chapters 1–3)

The published version of chapters 1–3 is a revised edition. Its narrative mechanisms draw on public-domain Chinese classical fiction: the old handkerchief in *Dream of the Red Chamber* (an ordinary object that carries a private relationship), *The Handan Pillow / 枕中记* (a full life lived in the time it takes to cook a meal), and *Xi Fangping / 席方平* (an appeal that must pass successive counters, each exacting a cost). These are structural influences, not copied text: no modern translation, annotation, commentary or adaptation was reproduced. The work remains original speculative fiction in English.

The revision was drafted with AI assistance. The decision to publish it was made by apprank studio on 2026-11-23; no sentence-by-sentence human rewrite is claimed. The earlier English text is preserved in the source repository.

Because the English source changed, the earlier Turkish, Cantonese and Korean approvals were invalidated. On 2026-11-23 the user instructed "全部发布" to release all four languages of this edition. The three translations were re-created from the new approved English source, verified against its `source_sha256`, and given fresh independent approval records. All three languages — Turkish (`/tr/`), Cantonese (`/yue/`) and Korean (`/ko/`) — are now published alongside the English text and carry the same AI-assistance disclosure. The earlier translation versions are retained in the source history. Borrowing classical narrative mechanisms does not by itself guarantee compliance; the work is published on the basis of its originality and reader value, as recorded in the human review log.

Chapter 10, "The Rule, Written Down," transforms two public-domain conventions: an ordinary written object that carries a private relationship legible only to the people involved, and a formal appeal that cannot succeed before a single authority, requiring a named second witness. No modern translation, annotation, commentary, or adaptation was copied.

Chapter 11, "Two at the Loom," draws on the public-domain convention of two witnesses whose partial, mutually correcting accounts must be set side by side before a contested event can be recorded truthfully — the shared testimony that resolves a case in classical Chinese strange-tale and case fiction. Here that becomes a second person steadying a damaged engram so the missing interval is filled from another person's recollection rather than synthetic material. The blue door opening onto an ordinary room continues the motif of a much-feared revelation proving mundane once faced. No modern translation, annotation, commentary, or adaptation was copied.

Chapter 12, "Tell Me What She Actually Did," draws on two public-domain forms: the short anecdote preserved as exchanged speech, in which a visit is recorded through what was said and what was left unsaid without a narrator's verdict (as in *Shishuo Xinyu / 世说新语*), and the repeat visit in which the same event is told again with one detail altered, so the change lies in the telling rather than in the facts. Here the old man returns a third time asking for the same repair, and Elias refuses to fill the missing sound; instead he records the man's uncertain, self-correcting account in the margin, dated and attributed to the speaker, and leaves the seam visible. The chapter ends on an unfinished sentence rather than a resolution. No modern translation, annotation, commentary, or adaptation was copied.
