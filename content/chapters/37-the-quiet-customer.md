# The Quiet Customer

Entry 37 — 9 April. A woman sat at the counter for an hour and did not say what she wanted.

I have learned to let that happen. The trade is mostly listening to people describe a room they can no longer describe properly. She had a core in a cloth bag and no appointment, and at the end of the hour she said she wanted to know whether her memory of her sister was hers.

That is not a repair. It is a provenance question, and the trade has no instrument for it: a core records a viewpoint, the viewpoint is the customer's by definition, and the sister is not in the room to disagree.

I could have run a comparison against the sister's core if the sister had one and had consented. She did not. So I wrote the question in the ledger as an entry with no work attached, and told her the honest answer, which is that the trade can tell her the memory is stable and cannot tell her it is hers.

She paid nothing and thanked me. The entry is five lines and I think about it more than the paid ones.
