# The Grey at the Edge

Entry 27 — 27 January. Three cores this month came in with grey at the edge and no damage at all.

The grey — the patch the Ward will not name — has always been a symptom of damage in the trade's textbooks. These three were clean: no seam, no beat, no fill, nothing but a soft grey border where the memory should have ended in a clean frame.

One belonged to a bus driver, one to a school secretary, one to a woman who could not tell me what she did for a living. All three were in the same district. All three had moved there within the last two years.

I wrote the addresses in the ledger, because a pattern is the only evidence this trade can produce, and because a list of addresses is not a diagnosis and nobody can be reviewed for keeping one.

Mara read the page and said: a map. I said: a record. She said those are the same thing when the office is deciding who to blame.
