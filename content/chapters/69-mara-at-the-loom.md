# Mara at the Loom

Entry 69 — 14 November. Mara ran the loom, and by the rule the entry was written before the run rather than after.

She had not touched it since 2017 except to sign. The customer was one of hers from the old days, a woman with a core repaired badly in 2015 that had been drifting for a decade, and Mara asked to run it herself with me as the second hand.

The rule that requires a joint named before the loom is also a rule about the record: the ledger entry exists before the repair, so that a decision cannot be described after the fact as something else.

I wrote it at nine in the morning: joint named, second hand E. Vane, first hand M. (licence 4-119, conditional), customer's account read twice, price agreed, risk stated.

She ran it at eleven. It held. She came out and stood in the front room for a while and then said the thing I have been waiting four parts to hear: it is a better trade than the one I lost my licence in, and I do not know whether that excuses the ten years.
