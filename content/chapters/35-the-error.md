# The Error

Entry 35 — 2 April. Wren made an error, and it was the best thing that has happened this year.

A courier brought a core with a torn edge, four minutes of a kitchen, and no second account on file. Wren bridged the tear, closed the case, and wrote it up as a single-account repair because the customer had signed alone.

The rule does not allow that. A single-account repair is allowed only when the second account is recorded as absent, with a reason. She had written absent, and the reason she gave was the customer's own words, which did not say absent, they said nobody else was there.

I found it in the evening when I read the day's pages, which is my job and the reason the book has two hands in it. The core had been repaired correctly. The record was wrong, and by the rule, a wrong record is the defect.
