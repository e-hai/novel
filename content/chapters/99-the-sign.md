# The Sign

Entry 99 — 16 June. The sign came down on a Monday. It went back up on the Friday with one line changed.

The old line was: repairs, bridges, fill, by appointment. The new line is: repairs and records, two hands.

The painter asked whether records were a thing a shop sold. Wren said no, they are a thing a shop keeps. He painted it and did not charge for the second line, which is the only free thing in this book offered by somebody who was not a customer.

We have had nine enquiries since Friday and four of them were about records, which is a higher proportion than any month this year. One was a woman who wanted to know whether her street was on a list. I said it was. She asked to see the page. I showed her.

That is the whole of the shop's policy now: if it is on the list, you can read the list. The office will have something to say about that in the autumn, and I have written the date of this entry down twice, once here and once on the back of the price card, in case the two pages disagree.
