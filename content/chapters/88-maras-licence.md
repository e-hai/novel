# Mara's Licence

Entry 88 — 31 March. The second inspector proposed lifting the condition on Mara's licence, on the grounds that nine years of compliance had demonstrated the rule rather than the licensee.

Mara asked her not to. In writing, four lines, which I have copied into the ledger because it is the only document in this book that a customer wrote about themselves.

She wrote: the condition is the only reason two hands are in this shop; if it is lifted the shop will become a one-hand shop within a year, because one hand is cheaper; the condition is not a punishment, it is a design.

The inspector withdrew the proposal on the eleventh of April and wrote back one line: noted, and I have not met a licensee who asked to keep a condition. Mara said the word she used in her reply was design and that she had chosen it in the evening and kept it.

The fourth condition — the licensee's own account shall not be repaired where she holds the second hand — remains. It remains because of me, and both of us know it, and neither of us has written that down until this entry.
