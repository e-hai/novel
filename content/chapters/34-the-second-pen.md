# The Second Pen

Entry 34 — 21 March. The second pen is not a signature. It is an hour of reading before the loom, and a page of notes that the customer never sees.

I gave Wren her first core to read on her own. She took forty minutes and came back with three questions: whether the customer had been told what the repair would cost in hours, whether the second account had consented, and whether the grey at the edge was in the original or an artefact of the case.

Two of the three I had not asked. The third is a defect in the way the core was stored, not the memory, and she was right to catch it.

The arithmetic: a repair that used to take a morning now takes a day and a half, and the shop does fewer of them, and the ones it does are better. The ledger for March has nine entries against nineteen in October, and I have stopped reading the October page, because it is not a comparison, it is a confession.
