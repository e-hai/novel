# Wren Asks About Mara

Entry 39 — 24 April. Wren asked why Mara signs.

I told her the version in the file: licence 4-119, suspended, reinstated conditioned on dual-account repairs, joint named before the loom. Wren said that was the version in the file and asked why she signs.

The true answer is shorter and worse. Mara signs because she was the one who repaired without a second account for nine years and cannot now claim she did not know what it cost. The signature is not a punishment. It is the only way she has found to be in the room without pretending she was never the reason the rule exists.

Wren thought about that for a while and then asked whether I sign for the same reason. I said no. I sign because the rule is correct and because I have been wrong in a way that the rule would have caught, and she wrote that down without comment, which is the highest compliment an apprentice has paid me.
