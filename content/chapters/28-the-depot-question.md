# The Depot Question

Entry 28 — 2 February. A letter from the Ward, second class, asking whether the shop has observed any change in the incidence of unclassified grey.

They know. They have the returns from every licensed shop in the district, and they are asking the smallest shop on Cinder Lane to put in writing what the returns already say, which is what an institution does when it wants a sentence it can quote later.

I wrote four lines: incidence has increased; the increase is concentrated in one district; the affected cores show no damage; the shop has no diagnosis and will not offer one.

Mara signed under it without reading it twice, which she has never done before. Then she said the interesting part was not the grey. It was that the Ward had used the word observed, and that in the trade's vocabulary observe has a cost attached.

I looked it up that evening. She was right. An observation is evidence. A diagnosis is liability.
