# The Second Inspector

Entry 76 — 6 January. The second inspector came on the sixth, a woman of about fifty with a folder and no folder for the shop.

She is reviewing the review of the review, which is a sentence the Ward uses without embarrassment. She read the shop's nine questions before she read Ader's file and then asked the question no one has asked since 2015: what does the shop want to happen.

Not what does the shop recommend. What does it want. I said the shop wanted Ader's findings to be readable by somebody other than the board, and that wanting it was not a procedure, and she said that was the first honest answer she had had all month.

She wrote one page and left it with us: a proposal that findings be filed with the names of everyone in the room. The board will reject it in March. She knows. She filed it anyway, which is what a person does when a procedure is the only thing they have.
