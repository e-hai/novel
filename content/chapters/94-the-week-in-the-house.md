# The Week in the House

Entry 94 — 12 May. The week in the house was not the week I have been telling myself.

In the account: a woman, a child, a kitchen, four days of a rainfall that flooded the lane, and a repair done on the fifth day to remove the child's name from a document inside her memory. The repair was a legal single-account repair because the child's second account did not exist yet.

In the ledger for 2015, page 4: the same repair, same date, one signature, hours 6, fee paid, and a note in my own hand that I have read four times this week: customer asked me not to record the child.

I did not record the child. I also did not record the request. Twenty thousand words of this book about the trade's records, and the first thing the trade ever asked me to leave out is in my own handwriting as a courtesy.

Mara read it and said: that is a seam, Elias. A page that used to be there, and a line that says it was. I said I know. She said: no, you do not, you are about to write a book about it instead.
