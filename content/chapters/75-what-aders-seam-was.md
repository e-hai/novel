# What Ader's Seam Was

Entry 75 — 23 December. I read the seam. It is on page 84 and it is nine minutes long.

The memory is a corridor in a building Ader has never described to anyone, and a door at the end of it, and a person standing at the door whose face is not in the account. The repair has bridged the face and left the corridor, the door, and the standing.

So the finding he wrote was a finding about a room he could not look at, and the repair he made was to remove the person from it, and the log he showed the reviewed party showed the hours and not the subject.

Nine minutes. It took me forty minutes to read and I have read it twice since. The trade's whole argument is in that seam: a repair can be honest, licensed, logged, countersigned, and still be a way of not looking at a person.

I have not put that in the questions to the board. I have put it in this ledger, under the date, where it will be read by whoever reads the book.
