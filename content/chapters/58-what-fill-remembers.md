# What Fill Remembers

Entry 58 — 1 September. Fill carries weather.

The trade says fill has no taste: it holds a shape and carries no feeling. We tested nine lots against the donor's own cores, where they existed, and found the donor's weather in seven: the temperature, the light, and in two cases a smell.

Not feeling. Weather. The difference matters, because a customer who has been filled can tell you the afternoon was cold when their own afternoon was warm, and they cannot say why they know, and the trade has been calling that a bridge artefact for thirty years.

Wren asked whether we should tell the customers. I said we should tell the ones who ask, and that the honest version of the trade is smaller than the one on the counter card, and that I have been selling a larger one since I was twenty-six.

The entry is long. It is the first one in this book I have written twice, because the first version was an accusation and the second is a finding.
