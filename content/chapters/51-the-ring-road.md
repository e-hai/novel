# The Ring Road

Entry 51 — 14 July. The warehouse is a shed behind a fence outside the ring road, and it is the least mysterious place in this book.

Two men, a weighbridge, a printer, a shelf of stamped lots, and a laminated sign about returns. Fill comes in by the brick: a brick is four hours of somebody's afternoon, weighed, compressed, stamped with a lot number and a date, and priced by the hour with a discount above forty.

I went to look and not to buy. The man at the weighbridge asked what I wanted. I said I was a licensed repairman. He said licensed repairmen get the discount and do not get to ask about the donor.

That sentence is the whole of Part IX. I wrote it down in the car park, in the ledger, with the date at the top, and it is the first entry in this book made outside the shop.
