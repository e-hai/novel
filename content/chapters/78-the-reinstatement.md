# The Reinstatement

Entry 78 — 20 January. The board reinstated Ader on the twentieth, without conditions, with a note that the review of the review had found no procedural defect.

No procedural defect. There was no procedure for the thing he did, so there could be no defect in it, and the reinstatement is therefore correct and complete and says nothing at all.

The shop's nine questions were filed with the reinstatement as an appendix. Eight of them are unanswered in the text and the ninth is answered by a page reference that does not exist in the copy we received.

Mara read the reinstatement twice and said: this is what the office looks like from inside a rule that has no category for people. Wren said: then the categories are the problem. Both are right and neither is a finding.

I have kept the file. The shop keeps files it cannot use, which is not procedure either, and this one is on the shelf with the unopened letter, and the two of them together are the whole of Part XI.
