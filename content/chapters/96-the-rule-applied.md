# The Rule Applied

Entry 96 — 26 May. We applied the rule to the shop, which nobody had done, including us.

Two hands on every core, the joint named before the loom, both names in the ledger, and now a fourth: no entry about the shop may be signed by one person. Wren proposed the fourth in April and it is in force as of this week.

The first entry under the new rule was about the shop's own income: nine hundred and forty in May against eleven hundred in April, two thousand hours unbilled this year, and a bank that has asked four times what the shop is.

It is a short page and it is the hardest one to sign, because it says the shop is running down and the file is growing and neither of those is a repair.

Mara said, signing: a rule that survives being applied to the people who wrote it is a rule. A rule that does not is a policy. I have written both sentences into the entry and I do not know which one describes this shop.

Wren, at the bottom: both. And that is why the fourth condition exists.
