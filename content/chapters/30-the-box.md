# The Box

Entry 30 — 24 February. The ledger came back in its box with a stamp on the inside cover and a note in the Ward's hand.

The stamp says the book has been examined and returned. The note says two things. First, that the column of unbillable hours is not a defect and may be kept. Second, that one page has been removed under the examination rules: the page of addresses.

They kept the map. I kept the record of having made one — the entry above the removed page still says three cores, one district, addresses written, and the page is gone, so the ledger now attests to a document that is not in it.

Mara read the gap and said: that is what a seam looks like from the outside. A page that used to be there, and a line that says it was.

End of Part VI. Eleven refusals, forty-one entries, one missing page, and the shop still open.
