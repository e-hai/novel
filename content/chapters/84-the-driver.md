# The Driver

Entry 84 — 3 March. The bus driver came back. His core was one of the three clean ones with grey at the edge, in January, and I turned him away because there was nothing to repair.

He has been driving the 14 for sixteen years and the route was cancelled in March and reissued in July under a different number, and he has kept a notebook of what passengers say when they get on.

Four hundred and eleven entries. Things like: does this bus go to the hospital, and the answer is yes. Things like: I waited forty minutes, and the answer is no you did not. Things like: where is the shoeshop, and he has written, in the margin, that there has not been a shoeshop on that street since April.

He brought the notebook because he thought the shop should have it. I asked why. He said because the shop is the only place in the district that keeps things that are true and does not sell them.

The notebook is in the ledger now, in the sense that it is cited in an entry. I have not taken it. He took it home and will bring it back in a year.
