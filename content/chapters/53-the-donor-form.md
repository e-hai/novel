# The Donor Form

Entry 53 — 28 July. The donor form has two boxes. One for the donor, one for the witness. The witness box is empty on every form in the shelf.

A donor cannot witness their own donation, which is correct. A warehouse cannot witness a donation it sells, which is also correct. So the second box is filled by a clerk, and the clerk is paid by the warehouse, and the form attests that the donation was freely given by a person who was in the room.

I asked the clerk at the weighbridge how many forms she signs a day. She said ninety. I asked whether she reads them. She said she reads the top line, which is the name, and that she has been doing this for six years and has never met a donor.

Mara's comment, when I showed her the form: this is the shop's problem moved upstream. A second signature that costs nothing is not a second signature. I have copied that sentence into the ledger twice, once on the day she said it and once when I understood it.
