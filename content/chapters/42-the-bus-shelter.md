# The Bus Shelter

Entry 42 — 12 May. The bus shelter on the ring road has a timetable in it for the 14, which was cancelled in March and has not run since.

Six people have reported waiting for it. Two of them waited for forty minutes. The panel is paper, laminated, and the 14 is printed on it in the same type as the routes that do run.

I have a rule about paper: paper is a record, and a record can be wrong, and a wrong record is not the same as a false one. The 14 was real in March and is not real now, and the timetable has not been told.

Wren wanted to know whether that was the grey or just a council that had not reprinted a panel. I said the difference was the waiting, which is not an answer, and she wrote it in the ledger as a question rather than an observation.

The district office has since removed the panel. The waiting has not stopped. Eight people this week.
