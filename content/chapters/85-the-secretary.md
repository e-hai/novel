# The Secretary

Entry 85 — 10 March. The school secretary came with a list of nine children who had stopped using the north corridor and a note from each parent.

The corridor has not been repaired, because a corridor is not a patient. The children have found their own repair: they go the long way, and the long way has become the way, and two of them have stopped talking about it, which is the trade's definition of a memory that has been managed rather than repaired.

She wanted to know whether the shop could certify the corridor as damaged so that the district would resurface it. The trade has no instrument that certifies a building, and the Ward's guidance forbids a shop from diagnosing a district.

So the shop cannot help, and the secretary has done the thing that institutions do not do: she kept the list, and the list is a record, and it says nine children, four months, one corridor.

I have copied her list into the ledger as an appendix to entry 85, with her name on it, which is the first time in this book that a customer's own record is filed inside the shop's.
