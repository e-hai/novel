# The Silence

Entry 68 — 7 November. After the ninth dual run the shop was silent for four days.

Not closed. Silent: no loom, no customers taken, the two of us reading and re-reading the pages, and Wren keeping the front room. A dual run takes more out of the people than out of the cores, and after nine of them in seven weeks we had begun to make the kind of decision that a joint signature will not catch.

Wren wrote the entry for those four days, and it is the only page in the ledger that is about the shop rather than about a customer. It says: the shop stopped, on purpose, and the reason is in the pages before this one, and the reason is that both hands were tired at the same time.

That is the whole entry. Four lines. Mara read it and said it was the most useful page in the book, because a business that cannot record its own fatigue will record its mistakes as findings.

I have nothing to add to it.
