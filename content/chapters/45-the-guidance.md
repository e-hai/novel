# The Guidance

Entry 45 — 2 June. The Ward's guidance arrived: four pages, no diagnosis, one instruction.

It says that unclassified grey is to be recorded and not repaired; that shops should log addresses where three or more instances coincide; that no shop shall offer a diagnosis of a district; that the guidance does not constitute an admission that a district-level phenomenon exists.

Four pages to say: we know, we will not name it, and you will keep our records for us.

Mara read it and said the last clause is the important one, because a document that denies its own subject cannot be quoted against the office that wrote it. Wren read it and said the first clause is the important one, because it forbids the only repair that would help.

I comply. I have been complying since February, and the ledger has forty addresses in it, and the guidance now has a use for them that I did not have when I started writing them down.
