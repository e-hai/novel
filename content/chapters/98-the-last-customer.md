# The Last Customer

Entry 98 — 9 June. The last customer of the year was a girl of nine with a core in a school bag and eleven pounds.

She had a memory of a bus, a route number, and a driver who told her where to get off, and she wanted to know whether it was real. Her mother had told her the route did not exist. She had eleven pounds, which is the price of a bridge, not a reading.

We did the reading. Two hands, no fee: forty minutes, three addresses, one bus driver's notebook from March, and a page number that named the 14 and the driver and the hour she got on.

She paid the eleven pounds anyway and Wren wrote the receipt, and it is the only receipt in this book made out to a child.

Her memory was real. The route was cancelled in March and reissued in July as the 41, and the driver has been driving it for sixteen years. The record says so, and the record is not for sale, and she has it now, which is the entire purpose of a file.
