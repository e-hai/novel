# A Hundred Addresses

Entry 50 — 7 July. A hundred and four addresses. The Ward has asked for the list, in writing, for the second time this year.

The first time they took a page out of the book. This time they want the whole map, and they have asked in the language of a request that is not one: for the purposes of district planning.

I gave them a copy and kept the original, which is the first thing in this book that I have done as a matter of policy rather than of habit. A copy can be checked against the original. An original handed over is a document that only one office can produce.

The copy went out on the ninth. On the eleventh, two streets on it were closed for resurfacing, and one of them had been resurfaced in April. I do not know whether that is a coincidence and I have written that I do not know, which is the only honest line available to me.

End of Part VIII. A hundred and four addresses, nine filled cores, one street photographed four times, and a map that is now in two places.
