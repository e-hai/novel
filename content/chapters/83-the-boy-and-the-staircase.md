# The Boy and the Staircase

Entry 83 — 24 February. The boy from 2014 came back with his mother, which is the first time in this book that anyone has come back after eleven years.

He asked, at nine, for a staircase to be taken out of a memory of a house — the staircase to a room where something happened that he was not told about and does not now remember.

He is twenty-one. He climbs that staircase every day. It is in his parents' house and he does not know the repair exists, and his mother has never told him, and he came in because he had begun to dream about the house in a way that did not match the house.

The repair worked. That is the testimony: it worked perfectly, and a man has spent eleven years climbing a staircase that he asked to have removed, and the record says the repair was correct.

His mother asked whether we would tell him. The rule says the shop may not disclose a repair to a third party, and may disclose to the customer, and he is the customer, and he had forgotten.

I did not answer the mother's question. I have written it in the ledger, under the date, and I have not made a decision about it.
