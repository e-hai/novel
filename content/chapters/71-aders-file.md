# Ader's File

Entry 71 — 28 November. A file arrived from the board with a covering note: for review, as the reviewed party.

Inspector Ader repaired his own doubt in order to write finding 12/2026. He did it legally — an auditor may repair his own doubt provided the log is shown to the reviewed party — and the log was shown, and the board now wants the shop's opinion of the result.

Two hundred and eleven pages. Three accounts of the same audit, taken at three shops, one of them this one. A seam in the third account that is not in the first two. A note in his own hand on page 84: I do not remember writing this page and I am signing it anyway.

I have read a great many cores in this trade and I have never read a file that was so plainly a confession with a licence number on it.

Mara read it in one sitting and said: this is what the rule looks like when it is applied to a man who wrote the rule. I wrote that down and then wrote the date at the top, which is the office's rule, and which I have stopped finding funny.
