# The Woman Whose Seam Held

Entry 82 — 17 February. The woman from January came back, the one with the eleven-year seam and the week in a house that no longer exists.

She has not had the seam repaired. She has, in the year since, started writing the week down — the house, the rain, what she can see of it — and has found that a written page can hold what a bridged memory cannot.

She says the shop's repair was the best work she has ever paid for and she would not undo it. She also says she has stopped trusting repairs entirely, which is not a contradiction, and I have let it stand in the ledger without smoothing it.

Mara read the entry and said this is the difference between a customer and a case, and that the trade has no column for it. We added one that week. It is called testimony and it is the only column in this book with no numbers in it.
