# The Loom at Night

Entry 38 — 17 April. Wren ran the loom alone at night, with the door locked and the log open, and I sat in the front room and did not go in.

A loom does not need two people. What needs two people is the decision, and the decision was made before she started: core, joint named, second account read, price agreed. She was executing, which is the only part of this trade that can be delegated.

She came out at eleven with a page of notes and one question: whether the customer's fill had been weighed by the yard or by the hour, because the lot number did not say. I told her the lot numbers never say, and that this is the trade's oldest cheat, and she wrote that down as a defect of the supplier rather than of the shop.

The night hours go into the ledger now. The shop bills for daylight and keeps the dark ones, which is not a policy, it is how the arithmetic came out when the rule started.
