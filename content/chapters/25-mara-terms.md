# Mara's Terms

Entry 25 — 4 January. Mara came in before opening and put three conditions on the counter.

First: she reads every core before I touch it, not after. Second: if she refuses, the refusal goes in the ledger with her reason and not with mine. Third, and she said this looking at the window rather than at me: if the core belongs to me, she signs, she does not read.

I asked what the difference was. She said reading a man's own memory is not a review, it is a diagnosis, and she is not qualified to diagnose me, only to countersign.

I wrote all three into the log. The third one is the only entry in a hundred pages that I have read more than once, and I still do not know whether it is a kindness or a limit.
