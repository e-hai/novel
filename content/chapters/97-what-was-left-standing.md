# What Was Left Standing

Entry 97 — 2 June. The trade closes a core by deciding what to leave standing, and in June the shop did it to itself.

What is left standing: the bench, because Wren can run it and the district needs a repairman who reads twice. The file, because it is the only thing in the district that says what happened in the grey streets without selling it. The ledger, because a file without a record is a cupboard. And Mara's fourth condition, because it is the reason the other three are checked by someone.

What is not left standing: the price list as it was. The shop has been charging for the bench and giving away the reading, and from June it charges for the reading and gives the bench at cost, which is the only way the arithmetic works when the reading is the product.

That is the whole decision, and it took a year and a page. Wren wrote the price card out by hand because the printer wanted two weeks.

Mara read the card and said: nobody will pay for reading. I said they will pay for the truth about a room. She said: nobody has ever paid for that either, and she signed the card anyway.
