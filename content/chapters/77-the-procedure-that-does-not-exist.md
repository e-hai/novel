# The Procedure That Does Not Exist

Entry 77 — 13 January. We drafted it. Four pages, eleven clauses, one of them the only clause that matters: a reviewer who has repaired his own account shall declare the subject of the repair before the finding is written.

Mara wrote the first draft and I edited it and Wren typed it, which makes it the only document in this book with three hands on it. We sent it to the board and to the second inspector and to nobody else.

The board's reply came in eleven days: received, noted, not adopted, and filed under guidance submissions. That is the Ward's way of saying literature.

Ader's reply came in nine and I have not opened it. It is on the shelf above the ledger in the shop, unopened, and it has been there for six weeks, and I have written in the book that it is there, because an unopened letter in a shop is a thing that somebody will eventually open and it will not be me.

Wren asked if she could open it. I said not yet, and she wrote that down too, which is the apprentice's privilege and the reason her pages are better than mine.
