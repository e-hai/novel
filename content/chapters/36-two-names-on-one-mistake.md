# Two Names on One Mistake

Entry 36 — 3 April. The mistake is filed under both names, which is what the rule says and not what I wanted.

A joint signature means two people own the record. Wren's error is therefore mine: I read the page at the end of the day instead of before the loom, and the rule was written to prevent exactly that, and I signed the shop's name to a repair I had not signed for.

The Ward's form has a box for this. It is called an error of record, and the shop has never ticked it in eleven years. I ticked it, wrote four lines, and sent the shop's first self-report to the district office.

Mara read the copy and said, without any pleasure at all: now the ledger is a record of the shop and not only of the customers. She is right, and the pages after April read differently, because they are written by people who know they are being read.
