# The Summer Donated Twice

Entry 55 — 11 August. Lot 8841 and lot 8902 contain the same summer.

Not similar. The same: the same dog, the same fortnight at the same coast, the same wedding with the same band playing the same wrong note in the second bar. One lot was stamped in June and one in July, and the donor form for the second names a donor who is not the donor on the first.

Somebody sold a summer twice. This is the first thing in this book that is straightforwardly a crime, and it is not a crime the Ward has a procedure for, because the Ward regulates repair and not supply.

I sent the two lot sheets to the district office with a covering line. They replied in nine days asking whether the shop wished to make a complaint. The answer is yes, and the answer is also that a complaint from a licensed repairman about a supplier is a review of the repairman, because the supplier's contract is with the Ward.

I made the complaint. The ledger number for it is 55, and it is the only entry in the book with a second page.
