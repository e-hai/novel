# The Street That Photographs Wrong

Entry 44 — 26 May. A street on the southern edge has been repaved twice, and in photographs the old cobbles show through.

This is not the trade. This is a street, a camera, and something between them. But it is the fourth address in the district with the same signature — late sound, absent duration, an image that disagrees with the ground — and I have started carrying a camera, which is a thing a repairman should never have to own.

I photographed the street at four hours of the day and got four different pavements. The camera is a machine that records a viewpoint, so the photographs are honest and the street is honest and the disagreement is in neither of them.

Wren asked what a repair would even mean here. I said a repair would mean deciding which pavement the street is allowed to have, and that this is the same question as a seam, one scale up, and that no one has licensed the shop for a street.
