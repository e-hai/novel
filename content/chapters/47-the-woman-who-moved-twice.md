# The Woman Who Moved Twice

Entry 47 — 16 June. The woman who could not tell me what she did for a living came back with a second core.

She moved into the district in March, out in April, and back in May. She has two memories of the same street: in the first the street is loud and the shop on the corner sells shoes; in the second it is quiet and the shop sells paper.

Both are accurate. The shoeshop closed on the ninth of April. The street went quiet at some point in the spring, and nobody can say when, and she is the only person in the district whose two memories bracket the change.

Wren read both cores and asked whether we could date the grey from them. I said we could date it to a fortnight, which is the first hard number anyone has produced, and that producing it would put the woman on a list.

She said the ledger already had her on one. She was right. It has had her on one since January, under an address and no name.
