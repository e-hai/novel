# The Empty Box

Entry 54 — 4 August. From Monday the shop does not use fill from lots with an unwitnessed donor form.

This is not a rule the Ward requires. It is a rule Mara and I wrote on a piece of paper and put in the front of the ledger, and it costs the shop about a third of its work, because a third of the shelf has an unwitnessed form.

The first customer to be affected was a man who wanted a bridge and could not afford the delay. He asked whether the fill was dangerous. I said it was not dangerous, it was unattested, and he said those are the same thing to a man with a damaged core and a job on Monday.

He was right. I did the repair with attested fill, which took four days and cost him more, and he paid and did not complain and has not come back.

Wren asked whether we had done him a service or a lecture. The entry does not answer that question. It records that we did it, the price, and the four days.
