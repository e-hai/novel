# The Receipt

Entry 60 — 12 September. A receipt arrived for lot 8902, forty hours, billed to the shop, dated the fourth of August.

The shop did not buy lot 8902. The receipt is on warehouse paper, it has the weighbridge's stamp, and it is signed by the clerk with the empty second box.

Somebody has billed the shop for the summer that was sold twice, three weeks after I sent the two lot sheets to the district office. I do not know whether that is an error, a message, or a test, and I have written all three possibilities into the entry because guessing would be worse than recording.

Mara's reading: it is a receipt for a complaint. The warehouse is telling the office that the shop bought the lot it complained about, which makes the shop's complaint a shop's problem rather than a supplier's.

I sent the receipt to the office with the covering line from entry 55. No answer yet.

End of Part IX. Two lot sheets, one notebook, ninety forms a day, and the shop's first receipt for something it did not buy.
