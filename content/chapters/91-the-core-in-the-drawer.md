# The Core in the Drawer

Entry 91 — 21 April. I took my own core out of the drawer.

It has been in the same drawer since 2015, wrapped in the cloth that came with it, and I have not opened the cloth. Two thousand and eleven sessions at this bench and the one core I have never read is mine.

What is on it: a week in 2015, a shop, a woman with a child, a repair I did without a second signature because the rule did not exist yet. That is all I know. I have not needed to know more, and not needing has been a policy for eleven years.

The fourth condition on Mara's licence forbids the shop from repairing my own account where she holds the second hand. It says nothing about reading.

I put it on the bench at nine in the morning and left it there for four hours without opening the cloth, which is the longest repair in this book and it is done entirely by not starting.
