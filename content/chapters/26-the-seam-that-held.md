# The Seam That Held

Entry 26 — 12 January. A woman brought back a core repaired in this shop in 2015, when I was twenty-six and faster than I am now.

The seam had held. Eleven years, no drift, no beat, no grey at the edge. By every measure the trade has, it was a good repair, and I said so, and she said that was the problem.

She had been nineteen when it was done. She was thirty now. The memory she had asked to be bridged was of a week in a house that no longer exists, and because the seam held, she had never been able to grow a different week in its place. The repair had done exactly what she asked.

Mara asked her what she wanted. She said she did not know, and that was why she had come. We did nothing that day. The entry is three lines and the fee is zero, and it is the first time the ledger records a repair that was correct and wrong at once.
