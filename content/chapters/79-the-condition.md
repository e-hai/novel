# The Condition

Entry 79 — 27 January. Mara's licence came up for its annual review and came back conditional again, which is what it has been for nine years.

The condition is unchanged: dual-account repairs, joint named before the loom, both names in the ledger. The reviewer added one line this year — that the licensee's own account shall not be repaired in a shop where she holds the second hand — which is a new clause and a small one and it is aimed at me.

Mara read it and laughed, which I have heard her do perhaps four times. She said the Ward has finally noticed that the two of us are the only people in the district who could repair each other, and has decided to forbid it in the least useful way possible.

I wrote the clause into the front of the ledger, under the conditions, where it joins the other three. The fourth condition is the first one that is about a person rather than a document, and it is the first one I have any intention of disobeying.
