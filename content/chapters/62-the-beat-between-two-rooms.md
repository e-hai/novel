# The Beat Between Two Rooms

Entry 62 — 26 September. The failure has a name now: a beat between two rooms.

When the frames disagree by more than a third of a second, the dual run leaves a beat in both accounts — a sound that arrives before its own source, in a room the customer did not describe. It is the same signature as the damage the trade repairs, produced by the repair.

We found it on the fourth run. The customer was a woman with two accounts of a hospital corridor and a son who had been in it. Both accounts were hers, taken six years apart, and she wanted them reconciled.

The run produced a corridor with a beat. She noticed it before we did — she said the floor arrived after her footsteps — and we stopped, wrote it up, and refunded the day.

The ledger entry is the first one where the shop records a technique failing in a way the trade has no word for. Wren's note at the bottom: the beat is not in either account, so it is in the loom.
