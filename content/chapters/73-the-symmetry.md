# The Symmetry

Entry 73 — 9 December. The symmetry is uncomfortable and I am recording it rather than resolving it.

He repaired his doubt and produced a finding. I repaired my memory, in 2015, and produced a shop. Both of us used the technique to make a decision possible; both of us are now unable to say whether the decision was ours.

The difference is that his repair was logged and mine was not, and the difference is not moral, it is administrative. A logged repair can be reviewed. An unlogged one is simply a fact about a man.

Mara's line: the Ward has no procedure for a reviewer who is also a customer, so it will do the only thing an institution can do with an unclassifiable person, which is to keep him and read him annually.

I have written her line into the book and put a line under it: and the shop will be the one reading him.
