# The Boy Who Counted

Entry 24 — 19 December. The boy came back with his father and asked to see the loom.

I have never let a customer into the back room, and I did, and he stood there for a while looking at the mechanism like a child looking at a clock he intends to take apart. Then he said he had counted the tiles again and there were forty-one, not the forty he remembered, and that was the thing he actually wanted repaired: not the ward, the arithmetic.

So we did a nine-minute repair with two signatures on it. Forty-one tiles. Nothing else touched. The father paid and did not look at me.

In the ledger I wrote the hours as a whole day, because the reading had taken one, and then added a line under it, which is not procedure: the smallest repair in the book, and the only one where the customer was in the room.
