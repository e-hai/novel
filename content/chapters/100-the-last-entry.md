# The Last Entry

Entry 100 — 23 June. This is the last entry in this book, and it is written before the shop's second year rather than at the end of anything.

What happened: the shop kept a rule, kept a file, and repaired fewer memories than it did when it was dishonest by omission. The bench still runs. Wren runs it three days a week. Mara signs everything that has two names on it. The grey is still in the district and the list has two hundred and eleven addresses and the office has not answered the letters.

What did not happen: nobody was cured. The man who cannot hear his mother still cannot. The boy climbs the staircase. The corridor has nine fewer children in it and the school has not resurfaced it.

The last page is the shortest because the trade ends where the record begins: a date, a joint, a decision, and what was left standing.

Signed by two hands, as the rule requires, and by the apprentice, who is nineteen and will be here when it is read.

Elias Vane, Cinder Lane, 23 June. The ledger is complete.
