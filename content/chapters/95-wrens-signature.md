# Wren's Signature

Entry 95 — 19 May. Wren signed as an equal for the first time, on a page about the shop and not about a customer.

The question was whether to keep the shop open. Her page says: the shop has been a file for eight months and a bench for eleven years; the file does not pay and the bench does; if the shop closes, the file goes into a cupboard and the cupboard is where records go to stop being read; if the shop stays open, the file stays in a room where somebody has to answer for it.

Six lines. Then her signature, and under it, in smaller writing: I am nineteen and I will be here when this is decided.

Mara signed second, and then wrote something I have not seen her write before: second hand concurs, conditionally. I signed third, which makes this the first entry in the book signed by three people and the only one signed by an apprentice first.

We kept the shop open. The bank wrote a fourth time in June and we answered it in July.
