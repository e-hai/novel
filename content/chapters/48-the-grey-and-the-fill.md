# The Grey and the Fill

Entry 48 — 23 June. The grey has started appearing at the join in filled cores.

Fill is borrowed, weighed, stamped, and it has no taste. The grey at a fill join is new: a border where the borrowed afternoon meets the customer's own, and it is not a seam, because a seam is a disagreement and this is an absence of one.

I tested nine filled cores from the warehouse lots and found it in seven. The lot numbers are consecutive, which means it is a batch, which means it is a supplier, which means the shop has a name for the thing at last and the name is a number.

Wren asked whether the warehouse knew. I said the warehouse weighs, it does not test. She asked whether that was a defence. It is not, and I wrote that in the ledger in the same sentence as the lot numbers, which is the closest this book comes to an accusation.
